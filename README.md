# Locust Data Analysis

Analysing the relation between Cyclone occurrence and Change in Locust attack warning level.

## Findings

**The recent spike in locust population and movement can be directly attributed to occurence of cyclones:**

1. Heavy rains from cyclones in 2018 created ideal conditions in the Empty Quarter, leading to three unnoticed locust generations.

2. Early 2019: Swarms spread to Yemen, Saudi Arabia, and southwest Iran, with widespread breeding and limited control.

3. Mid to late 2019: Sizable swarms formed along the Indo-Pakistan border due to extended monsoon rains; locusts also expanded from Yemen into the Horn of Africa.

4. Late 2019: Swarms reached Kenya and parts of the Red Sea coast, forming hopper bands and spreading further.

5. Early 2020: Locusts continued breeding across Ethiopia, Kenya, and Somalia, while some moved into Uganda, South Sudan, and southern Iran.
